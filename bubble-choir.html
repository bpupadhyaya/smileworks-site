About the game

Glowing bubbles drift up through a dusk sky. Pop each one and it sings a note of the song.

Ten original songs

Lullabies, waltzes, rainy-day tunes, marches and gentle grooves, each with three levels and three stars.

A jam that never ends

The Endless Jam writes music as you play and always stays musical. Free Jam has no failure at all.

Sound is the product

Every instrument is made in code: soft bell, marimba, pad, pluck and a warm choir “ooh”. Lovely with headphones.

Collect your choir

Unlock singers with their own voices and perks, and a daily lullaby to keep your streak.
