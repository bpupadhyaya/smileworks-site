Go! Giggle!
About the game

Tiny felt-and-paper dioramas. Drop a few silly gadgets, press Go!, and a chain reaction ends with a grin.

Silly gadgets

Boing pads, ramps, fans, conveyors, seesaws, bubbles, magnets, sticky jam and a cork cannon, each with its own sound.

Six cosy worlds

Sixty dioramas from a Cozy Kitchen to a Space Picnic, with stars for solving and for going the extra mile.

No penalty

If it goes wrong, the pear makes a small embarrassed noise and everything resets. Just try again.

Endless Oops Machine

Rounds keep coming, each a little more mischievous. Plus a daily gizmo and a sandbox playground.
