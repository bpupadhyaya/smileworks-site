About the game

Aim a bouncy seed into a pegboard garden and watch buds burst into chain-reaction blooms.

One-finger aim

Drag to set angle and power. A dotted guide shows your first bounce, so every shot is a choice, not a guess.

Chains and pots

Blooms set off their neighbours. Catch a sliding flower pot for a x2, or the tiny one for a glorious x5.

Grow your garden

Every run earns pollen that grows your own garden between runs, with upgrades and 60 Journey levels.

Daily pond

A fresh seeded challenge every day, with a streak and a result you can share.
